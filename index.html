I am a creative developer, interface designer, and digital craftsman obsessively focused on bridging the divide between high-end visual aesthetics and high-performance interactive engineering.

Working with modern WebGL pipelines, custom GLSL shaders, physics engines, and cinematic editorial art direction, I design experiences that defy the template era. My work celebrates deep negative space, tactile cursor interaction, and photographic lighting.
