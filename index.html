ABOUT ME

I TURN CURIOSITY INTO CREATION — Full stack developer, graphics designer and creative technologist.

Hey, I’m Sagnik Adhikary — a 4th-year B.Tech ECE student at FIEM and a full-stack developer who enjoys turning ideas into interactive, useful, and visually engaging digital experiences.

I started with curiosity about how things work and gradually found myself drawn toward web development, UI design, and creative technology. Today, I work mainly with JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, and Django, while continuously exploring new technologies and building real-world projects.

I’m also someone who enjoys the creative side of technology. From UI/UX and graphic design to branding and animations, I like making products that don't just work — they look and feel good too.

Beyond the screen, I’m a mountain lover, trekker, traveller, and someone who enjoys being on stage. I believe the best ideas often come when you step outside your comfort zone.

I'm currently focused on becoming a better full-stack developer and problem solver, while building projects that I'm genuinely proud of.
